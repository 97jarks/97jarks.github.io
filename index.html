About

About Me

I'm Jackson Barnes, a 21-year-old musician and composer currently studying cybersecurity at Appalachian State University.

I've been a musician for around 10 years, and video games have always been one of my biggest passions. Combining those two things and building a career composing music for games is what I'm working toward.

I love experimenting with different styles rather than locking myself into one sound. Whether a game needs something orchestral, energetic, emotional, weird, happy, or completely chaotic, I'm interested in finding the musical identity that fits the project.
