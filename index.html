/// ABOUT ME ///

I'm Jackson Barnes. I'm 21 years old and currently studying cybersecurity at Appalachian State University.

I've been a musician for about 10 years and have spent a huge chunk of that time writing, recording, producing, and generally messing around with music.

Video games have always been one of my biggest passions, so composing for games is the thing I'm trying to turn into my career. I like making music that feels like it actually belongs to the world of a game instead of just sitting behind it.

I don't really want to lock myself into one genre either. I like orchestral stuff, weird electronic music, happy music, heavy music, emotional stuff, dumb music, serious music, and whatever else makes sense for the game.
