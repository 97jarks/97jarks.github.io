/// GAMES ///

Corosica

GMTK GAME JAM 2026

Corosica was my first game jam and my first time making a full soundtrack as part of a game development team.

I only had about 4 days to make the soundtrack, so some of the mixes might be a little funky, but I'm really glad I got to work on it and learn from the experience.

I'm also really glad I got to work with the rest of the team. They're all very talented people, so definitely check out their work too!

/// ABOUT ME ///

I'm Jackson Barnes. I'm 21 years old and currently studying cybersecurity at Appalachian State University.

I've been a musician for about 10 years and have spent a huge chunk of that time writing, recording, producing, and experimenting with different kinds of music.

Video games have always been one of my biggest passions, so composing for games is what I'm trying to turn into my career.

I don't really want to lock myself into one genre. I like orchestral stuff, weird electronic music, happy music, heavy music, emotional stuff, dumb music, serious music, and pretty much anything else if it fits the game.
